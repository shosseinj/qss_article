\section{Results}\label{sec:results}
The primary result is the paired timestamp comparison on QSNN-v3. Clean validation establishes the victim's predictive performance; separate packet-grid controls delimit its interpretation. Full seed, overlap and grid tables are retained in the supplement.

\subsection{Clean validation of the hybrid classifier}
QSNN-v3 reaches 97.27\% $\pm$ 0.21\% validation accuracy and reported macro-F1 of 97.27\% $\pm$ 0.21\% across five training seeds [R1]. The sample SD is conditional on shared pretraining and split, as specified in Sec.~\ref{sec:protocol}; it is not attack-rate uncertainty. The preserved SNN validation reference reaches 98.13\% $\pm$ 0.24\% accuracy, a descriptive QSNN-minus-SNN difference of -0.86 percentage points. A matched validation-F1 comparison is unavailable.

The seed-42 wide4 classical frontend reaches 97.80\% validation accuracy with 38,122 parameters, compared with 97.06\% and 40,762 parameters for its hybrid continuation. Different training stages and budgets prevent attributing this difference to an isolated circuit component. The supplement retains individual accuracies, selected epochs and development records; no quantum accuracy advantage or official-test hybrid result is established.

\subsection{Matched timestamp attacks}
Each cell uses the same 100 joint clean-correct validation examples (seed 42), so one success equals one ASR percentage point. Figure~\ref{fig:matched} groups the candidate and forward conditions because their aggregate outcomes coincide; these conditions remain different resource definitions. At both radii, PGD succeeds on 3/100 examples. TEMP succeeds on 4/100 and 3/100 at 5\% and 10\% under either access control, versus 4/100 and 5/100 under elapsed-time calibration. The latter uses $Q=4{,}000$ for QSNN-v3, with a reported median TEMP/PGD runtime ratio of 0.978 [R3].

The paired comparison is determined by discordant examples. At 5\%, all controls have three shared successes, one TEMP-only success and 96 failures of both attacks ($p=1$). At 10\%, access matching has three shared successes and 97 failures of both ($p=1$); elapsed-time matching has three shared, two TEMP-only and 95 failures of both ($p=0.5$). There are no PGD-only successes in these cells. Thus two examples account for the largest numerical contrast; no matched condition establishes TEMP superiority, and nonsignificance does not prove equivalence.

For the compact SNN, candidate and forward matching produce no successes at either radius. Elapsed-time matching uses $Q=1{,}600$ and a median runtime ratio of 1.083, producing 0/100 versus 0/100 at 5\% and 0/100 PGD versus 1/100 TEMP at 10\% ($p=1$). These selected-population observations do not estimate full-set robust accuracy or general hybrid-versus-SNN robustness. The supplement preserves every matching condition, reported bootstrap differences and attributed distortion summaries.

\subsection{Separate packet-grid controls}
The historical grid study includes ConvNet (spiking \texttt{SimpleNet\_v2}) and uses different victims, capacity-one packets and its own 1,000-example joint clean-correct test manifest for each representation (Sec.~\ref{sec:protocol}). Under the two completed budgets, custom-SNN ASR spans 98.0--100\%, whereas the three reference architectures span 0--1.9\% (Fig.~\ref{fig:grid}). Clean test accuracy, measured on all 10,000 examples, is shown separately in Table~\ref{tab:grid_clean}; the supplement supplies all sixteen attack cells.

The integer custom SNN and spiking ResNet18 differ by only 0.02 percentage points in clean accuracy, yet by 99.7 and 97.9 ASR points under $B_\infty=1$ and $B_0=200$. Clean accuracy therefore does not summarize the failure profile under this recipe. Common settings do not guarantee equally strong optimization across architectures, and zero observed successes are not robustness certificates \cite{athalye2018obfuscated,croce2020reliable}. These are single-seed local controls, not values copied from external benchmarks or a representation ablation.

The grid report attributes passing reconstruction/prediction audits to all cells, but the underlying artifacts are absent from the supplied snapshot. The unresolved $B_0$ penalty discrepancy described in Sec.~\ref{sec:attacks} further limits replication claims [R4].
